Play

Generate a seed, start everything with one command, then play Hyrule and New York until both Ganon and Shredder are beaten.

Generate a seed

The player files are in players\: alttp.yaml (slot Link) and tmnt.yaml (slot Turtles).

cd C:\Project\tmntalttp\Archipelago
..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe Generate.py --player_files_path ..\players --outputpath ..\output\marathon

The result is output\marathon\AP_<seed>.zip. It holds the server file, both patch files and the spoiler log (with the playthrough). Keep the spoiler with the production team, off screen.

Start a session

cd C:\Project\tmntalttp
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\start_stream.ps1 -Seed output\marathon\AP_<seed>.zip

The script patches the ROMs if needed and then starts, in order:

  1. MultiServer on localhost:38281, in its own console window (host commands go there).
  2. SNI.
  3. BizHawk with ALttP and SNI's Lua connector (window "alttp-stream [SNES] - BizHawk").
  4. BizHawk with TMNT and lua\tmnt_stream.luases: Archipelago's connector plus the area menu (window "tmnt-stream [NES] - BizHawk").
  5. SNI Client and BizHawk Client, already connected.
  6. Two PopTracker windows: ALttP (slot Link) and TMNT (slot Turtles).

It stops if port 38281 is already in use. Parameters: -Port, -AlttpSlot, -TmntSlot, -EmuHawk, -PopTracker, -NoTracker.

Received items show in BizHawk's message area: "Got Rope Pack from Link", "Found Leonardo".

What happens when…

…youThen
beat an Area bossThe game moves on to the next Area. If its pass is missing, the menu opens and stays open until you load an unlocked Area.
lose every turtleGame over, then CONTINUE. Continues are unlimited (the ROM is patched); the Area restarts and your owned turtles are free again.
run out of missiles or ropesWith the pack you are refilled at once. Vanilla pickups still exist as bonus ammo.
rescue a captured turtle in the gameIf you do not own her, the client captures her again.
start a new game from the title screenThe menu opens; pick an Area.
beat a boss while the client is disconnectedIt is remembered in RAM and sent when the client comes back.
see "Connect the AP client" in the menuThe BizHawk Client is not connected yet.

The ALttP side

Standard Archipelago A Link to the Past through SNI: open mode, goal Ganon, vanilla entrances. Its chests hold turtles, passes and packs; TMNT's bosses and entrances hold its items (Hookshot, Flippers and Moon Pearl always do). Crystals are shuffled between dungeons as usual; the runner sees them on the map.
