How the two games connect

Turtles are items

You start with one turtle. The other three are captured until you find them, usually in Hyrule.

Areas are dungeons

Each TMNT Area opens with its Sewer Pass (and sometimes a tool). You pick the Area from an in-game menu.

Bosses hold Hyrule's items

TMNT's bosses, sewer entrances and two gifts in the Turtle Lair hold A Link to the Past items.

Two goals

Ganon in Hyrule and Shredder in the Technodrome. The seed is done when both are down.

Start here

1. Setup

Double-click install.cmd, point it to your two ROMs; it installs everything else.

2. Play

Double-click start.cmd: create a seed, start and check the session, stop it. Plus the rules of the TMNT side.

3. Stream

OBS scene collection, go mode panel, trackers and the pizza-wipe transition.

4. Help

Known problems and what to do when something does not connect.
